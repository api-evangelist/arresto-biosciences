# Arresto Biosciences

Arresto Biosciences, Inc. was a privately-held, development-stage biotechnology company headquartered in Palo Alto, California, focused on medicines to treat fibrotic diseases and cancer by targeting enzymes involved in the synthesis of the extracellular matrix. Its lead product was AB0024, a humanized monoclonal antibody targeting the human lysyl oxidase-like-2 (LOXL2) protein.

**Status: acquired / defunct.** Gilead Sciences (Nasdaq: GILD) acquired Arresto for $225 million — announced December 20, 2010, closed in Q1 2011. Arresto no longer operates as an independent entity.

**API surface: none.** Arresto never published a public API, developer portal, documentation, or SDKs. Its former domain (`arrestobio.com`) no longer resolves.

Investors: Kleiner Perkins Caufield & Byers, HealthCare Ventures, Northgate Capital, DAG Ventures, Abbott Biotech Ventures.
